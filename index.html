From the beginning

Our Story

It started on a January day in New York. Since then there have been two cats, a lot of snow, more mountains than we can count — and, finally, a home of our own.

  1. Jan 2021

    The day we met

    A cold January in New York City, 2021. Neither of us knew it yet, but that was the start of everything.

  2. Sep 2021

    Off to Salt Lake City

    Eight months later we packed up and moved across the country to Salt Lake City, Utah. A new city, a new chapter, and the two of us figuring it all out together.

  3. 31 Oct 2021

    Mango and Bean come home

    On Halloween we brought home Mango and Bean. We thought we were adopting them; it turns out they adopted us — and they've run the house ever since.

  4. The Utah years

    Mountains, snow and miles

    Utah is where we learned to snowboard together (and fell more times than we'd like to admit), rode every mountain we could reach, hiked the state's highest peaks and lined up for more races than we can count. Snowboarding and running became our thing — the hours side by side we love most.

  5. Aug 2024

    The proposal

    On the summit of Grandeur Peak, still catching our breath with the whole valley below us, Chris got down on one knee. Hallie said yes.

  6. Nov 2024

    We said “I do”

    We exchanged our vows in Salt Lake City — the city that had quietly become home.

  7. Nov 2026

    Coming home to celebrate

    Now we're bringing it all back to Hanoi and Vinh to celebrate with our families and friends. The best part of this chapter is having you there with us.

For guests travelling

Travel & Stay

Some of you are travelling a very long way to be with us, and that means more than we can say. Here's what we hope will make the journey easier.

Getting to Hanoi

Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) is about 45 minutes by taxi from the centre. MIPEC Palace is in Đống Đa district, close to the middle of the city. If you can arrive a day early, you'll have time to rest — and fit in a bowl of phở before the party.

Hanoi to Vinh

About 300 km south. Roughly 1 hour by air (HAN → VII), 6–7 hours by train, or 5–6 hours by coach or private car.

Where to stay

If you'd like suggestions for where to stay in Hanoi or Vinh, please get in touch with us directly — we're happy to help you find somewhere lovely.

The week in between

There are seven days between the two celebrations. If you can stay for the week, we'd love that — more time together, more good food, maybe a trip or two. Tell us and we'll plan it with you.

Questions & Answers

What should I wear?

Whatever makes you feel good — smart but comfortable is perfect. November in Hanoi and Vinh is mild and pleasant, though midday can be sunny. No suits or gowns needed (unless you want to!).

What time should I arrive?

Around 15–20 minutes before 11:00 is perfect — it gives you time to say hello and take photos with us. The reception begins at 11:00.

Can I bring my children?

Of course — little ones are very welcome! Just include them in your RSVP count so we can seat your whole family together.

What about gifts?

Honestly, you being there is the gift. Please don't feel you need to bring anything else.

What language will the reception be in?

Mostly Vietnamese — but don't worry. We'll stay close, translate whatever you need and make sure you meet everyone. Smiles and a raised glass work in every language.

What happens during the reception?

Guests arrive and take photos with us and our families, then there's a short ceremony followed by a long, happy lunch. Things usually wrap up in the early afternoon.

Can I come to both?

Absolutely — that would make us so happy. Just choose “both” on the RSVP form.
